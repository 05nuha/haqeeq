# HAQEEQ — 2:50 submission video plan (short film + generative AI + pitch + demo)

Goal: one video that (a) hooks with a short film that uses generative AI, (b) delivers the same pitch you will give live, (c) shows the real demo, and (d) scores against the official rubric.
**Check the portal first** for the maximum length, file size, format and language of the video submission. Everything below targets **2:50**.

## 2. Where the generative AI goes (and how to stay honest)

The challenge is about AI-made fakes, so use generative AI to **show how convincing a fake is** — then show HAQEEQ answering it. Rules:
- **Label every generated clip on screen:** "AI-generated · fictional". A trust product must not fool the viewer — and the label becomes part of the story.
- **Fictional only:** no real presenter likeness, no real channel/agency branding or logos, no real news claim. (Spreading fake news in the UAE carries heavy fines — keep the clip inside the submission, don't post it publicly.)
- **Don't present generative AI as your solution's AI.** For the "AI" rubric, the solution's AI is Gemini scam-text analysis (with Arabic explanations) plus the image engine. The generated clips are creative support — say so: "We used generative AI to create the fake you just saw."
- Name the tools you used on the closing card (AI tools are encouraged): e.g. "Generative video: [tool]. Analysis: Gemini."

**Clips to generate** (short, 6–8 s each; AI video tools often garble Arabic text, so add all text/notice graphics in the editor):
1. **Fake announcement clip** — fictional news presenter, plain studio, no logos:
   > "A fictional news presenter in a generic modern studio with a plain dark-blue backdrop and an empty screen behind them, no logos, no channel branding, speaking calmly to camera, medium shot, soft studio lighting, vertical 9:16, looks like a video forwarded in a phone chat. Leave the screen area blank."
   In the editor, composite our **doctored notice** (`assets/doctored/public_holiday_notice_doctored_date.png`) onto the blank screen and add the label "AI-generated · fictional". Voiceover line: "Breaking: the national holiday has been extended to twelve days."
2. **Phone-forward moment (optional b-roll):**
   > "Close-up of a hand holding a smartphone in a cosy living room, a family chat on the screen, thumb hovering over the forward button, warm evening light, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16."
3. **Logo sting (optional):** animate your logo (shield with Arabic letter Haa and a check mark) in a 3-second clean reveal on navy, no extra text.
Start generating **now** — renders take time and may need several tries. Check what your account allows (length/limits) before relying on it.

## 4. Narration for the rest (matches the live pitch — same words)

**0:30–0:45 · Problem (≈40 words)** — "That moment is what HAQEEQ is built for. In a survey of 1,000 UAE residents, seven in ten had encountered a scam, and about half of those who reported saw no action or didn't know the outcome. People already check. The loop isn't closed."

**0:45–1:45 · Demo (≈90 words, over screen recordings)** — "A resident forwards the image in the app they already use. The genuine notice: matches an available official reference. Even a plain screenshot, where WhatsApp has stripped every signature, still matches. Change one date and HAQEEQ flags the edit — the heat-map shows where. Scam wording is flagged too, with an explanation in Arabic from Gemini. Public teams see it all on the control center — and open data tells them where to focus the next campaign, by reports per hundred thousand residents. If there's no reliable match, the result is unverified — never 'fake'."
Recordings to capture: phone screen (genuine → screenshot → doctored → scam text); laptop (dashboard feed + map, forensic heat-map, ranking panel, Push correction — **only include the correction if it reached a real test phone**). Don't record in `?mock=1`.

**1:45–2:30 · Pitch (≈110 words)**
- *How it works + data (12 s):* "Images are checked against official references and compared for pixel edits; scam text by Gemini plus rules. Government open data — Bayanat's entity directory and UAE population statistics — gives us source and geographic context. Background data is simulated and the signing key is mocked."
- *Business (15 s):* "Residents use it free; the buyer is the public entity that owns the official source. Funding options — entity licence, programme funding or co-funding — are to be validated in a pilot. Our riskiest assumption: an issuer must maintain references and answer fast."
- *90-day pilot (18 s):* "A proposed 90-day pilot, not a national rollout: one sponsoring entity and about twenty vetted references; a controlled test with invited users; and by day 90 an evaluation of response time, safety and cost."

**2:30–2:50 · Close (on camera, both)** — "HAQEEQ: Make Trust Part of Every Forward. We ask TDRA and one government media partner to consider a scoped 90-day pilot. This is a working prototype and a proposal. Thank you." Closing card: logo · tools used · "No approvals or agreements yet."

## 5. Rubric checklist (is each point visible in the video?)

- [ ] **Alignment & impact (15):** the film shows a fake official announcement and the moment of decision; the stats show scale; the response path shows impact.
- [ ] **Data (15):** the per-100,000 ranking is on screen in the demo; sources named in the pitch (Bayanat, u.ae, area names) with honest limits.
- [ ] **AI (15):** Gemini's Arabic explanation is visible; say which tools were used; generative clips are labelled as illustration, not as the solution.
- [ ] **Feasibility & 90-day plan (20):** the 90-day section is spoken *and* shown as a table with owners and measures.
- [ ] **Market (15):** buyer, funding options, costs, biggest risk — named, no invented prices.
- [ ] **Pitch (20):** one clear story (film → problem → proof → plan → ask), subtitles, clean audio, ends on the ask.

## 6. Make the video match the live pitch

- Write the live pitch first; record the video with the **same wording** and the same slides. The video is the polished cut of the same story.
- In the live pitch, open with the film's first ~15–20 seconds (or one still from it) instead of reading slide 1.
- Same honesty lines in both: unverified never means false; background data simulated; signing key mocked; no approvals yet.

## 7. Production checklist

1. Fresh Meta token → test `hello` → test the screenshot beat → test correction delivery (before recording the demo).
2. `demo_clear.py` then `demo_seed.py`; dashboard at 1920×1080; no phone numbers visible.
3. Generate the AI clips (2–3 tries each); label them; keep a copy of prompts.
4. Shoot the film (phone on a steady surface, daylight, quiet room); record voiceover separately.
5. Record demo screens (2–3 takes); trim dead waiting time but don't edit results.
6. Edit in CapCut / iMovie / DaVinci; add subtitles; check total ≤ 2:50; export 1080p MP4 within the portal's limits.
7. Watch it once with the sound off (subtitles must carry it) and once with a stranger, then submit.

**Cautions**
- **Consistency is the hard part.** Generate one still per character first, then animate each shot from those stills (image-to-video) so faces and clothes match across shots.
- **Likeness rules differ by tool.** Many tools restrict uploading photos of real people. Check what yours allows; if it refuses, describe the look in words (hair, glasses, clothing) — "inspired by" is enough.
